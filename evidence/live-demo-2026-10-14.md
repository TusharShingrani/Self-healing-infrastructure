was at 09:43:48 UTC; the deliberate restart at about +153.4 s succeeded.

## Finding: guardrail pacing matters

The immediate app-1 repeat and the subsequent service-stop fault arrived
while three repair actions remained inside the 20-tick rate-limit window.
The repeated BLOCKED events explain the delays. These outcomes cannot be
described as zero-downtime prevention or fast service repair. The guide now
explains waiting for that window between independent demonstrations.
No guardrail was disabled to make the results look better.

## Human review and LLM evidence boundary

The new `tools/demo_advice.py` uses the production Advisor and Healer with
a simulated target. Fake-provider demonstrations showed:

- Pending advice: zero repairs.
- Approved low-risk advice: one simulated repair and verified health.
- Rejected advice: zero repairs.
- Approved high-risk advice: zero repairs and a separate pending proposal.

A real Gemini request also succeeded using `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`.
For the simulated stuck metrics agent, Gemini proposed no catalogue action
and requested human checks. The advice was rejected and no repair ran.
This proves the provider call and review flow, not an LLM-directed live VM
repair. Raw provider and approval outputs are adjacent evidence files.

## Final state

report both healthy. Prometheus reports every configured scrape target up.

running advisor (`healer_advisor_available 1`). Keys remain only in memory
and must be re-entered after a healer reboot. The keys were pasted into chat
